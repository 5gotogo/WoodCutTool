FAQ

Why use a contacts backup app?

It makes contact export repeatable and produces common file formats.

What formats matter?

CSV, Excel, and vCard are useful for backup, review, and migration.

Is manual export bad?

No. It is just easier to delay or do inconsistently.

When should I back up contacts?

Before switching phones, changing accounts, traveling, or cleaning up duplicate contacts.
