What this comparison covers

FAQ

Is a shift app better than a paper schedule?

For rotating shifts, yes. The app repeats patterns, sends reminders, and totals hours, which paper cannot.

Can the app calculate my hours?

Yes. It can total worked hours and overtime so you do not have to add them by hand.

Does it handle rotating shift patterns?

Yes. You set a pattern once and it repeats automatically across the calendar.

Will it remind me before a shift?

Yes. The app can send reminders so you do not miss or mistime a shift.
