FAQ

Is on-device photo cleanup safer?

It can be more privacy-friendly because photos do not need to be uploaded for review.

Should deletion be automatic?

Usually not. Similar photos often need human judgment.

When is a cloud cleaner okay?

When you trust the service and already manage photos in that cloud.

Who should use SnapCleaner?
