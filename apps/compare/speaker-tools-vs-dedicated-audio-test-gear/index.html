FAQ

Can Speaker Tools replace professional test gear?

No. It is for quick checks, not calibrated measurement.

When is dedicated gear necessary?

For professional calibration, installations, or precise room analysis.

Why use a phone app?

It is fast, portable, and good enough for many basic speaker checks.

Who should use Speaker Tools?

Home users, creators, musicians, and small teams doing quick audio checks.
