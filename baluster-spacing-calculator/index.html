FAQ

Common Baluster Spacing Calculator questions

What gap should I use between balusters?

Use the maximum opening required by the applicable local code; many residential projects use a value below 4 inches, but rules and measurement methods vary.

Does this account for sloped rails?

No. It is for a straight clear opening. A sloped rail needs layout verification along the rail and local-code review.
