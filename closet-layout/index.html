Survey the space

Find the usable envelope and the routes into it.

Measure a closet opening at three heightsRecord low, middle and high widths on the same reference plane and carry the tightest measured width into the plan. Map door tracks, trim and closet obstructionsTurn hinges, tracks, service access and trim into explicit keep-outs before setting panel depth. Budget closet fillers and baseboard clearanceReserve space for irregular walls and trim before assigning any clear bay widths. Check the delivery path and panel tilt-upScreen tall uprights against the transport route and the space needed to raise them.

Lay out the bays

Fit the items you store and preserve everyday access.

Size a single-hanging closet bayLay out a rail and shelf around the longest garments on the actual hangers. Fit two tiers of short hangingSet the rail levels against real garment drops and the movement needed to remove hangers. Plan adjustable linen shelves around real stacksSize clear shelf openings and reach depth for towels, folded bedding and bins. Align closet bays with bypass door openingsUse each door's actual parking positions to locate the dividers and keep wide bins removable. Treat walk-in closet corners as shared spaceAllocate each corner to one run and verify the clear aisle after handles and garments are in place.
