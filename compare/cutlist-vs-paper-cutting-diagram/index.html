Pros and cons

Tradeoffs by option.

CutList pros

  • Best fit for multi-sheet plywood projects, client changes, repeated parts, and PDF sharing
  • Revision: Easy to edit and re-run
  • Kerf: Included as input
  • Clarity: Part labels and layout
  • Clear choice when the project is designed around CutList.

CutList cons

  • Can be the wrong choice when the job is closer to early sketches, concept planning, and simple shop notes.
  • Requires checking real stock, tool setup, installation conditions, and finish expectations.
  • May cost more in rework if chosen only because CutList is familiar.
  • Compare against Paper diagram with the actual measurements before buying.

Paper diagram pros

Maintenance

Maintenance and long-term fit

Do not compare only sticker price or the first setup step. Compare the whole workflow: measuring, buying, cutting, installing, finishing, revising, maintaining, and repairing the result. A cheaper or faster option can still lose if it creates more waste, harder cuts, weaker fastening, worse appearance, or more rework after the first mistake.

Which is better: CutList or Paper diagram?

When should I choose CutList?

Choose CutList for multi-sheet plywood projects, client changes, repeated parts, and PDF sharing.

When should I choose Paper diagram?
