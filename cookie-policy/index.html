Cookie Policy

This page explains how WoodCutTool may use cookies or browser storage for site functionality, preferences, and links to third-party services.

Browser storage

WoodCutTool calculators are designed to run in your browser. Some tools or interface settings may use local browser storage to remember preferences such as language, recent inputs, or display state.

Calculator inputs

Calculator inputs are intended for planning and previewing results in the browser. Avoid entering confidential, sensitive, or regulated information into any public website calculator.

Third-party services

External services such as app stores, embedded translation tools, analytics, hosting providers, or linked websites may use their own cookies or tracking technologies under their own policies.

Your controls

You can clear cookies, local storage, or site data through your browser settings. Disabling storage may reset preferences or reduce some site functionality.
