How the estimate works

Use the number as a planning starting point.

Surface area multiplied by coats and waste allowance is divided by product coverage per gallon, then rounded up for a purchase estimate.

Measure first

Measure finished dimensions, actual stock, openings, and site constraints before ordering material.

Check the result

Coverage changes with wood species, weathering, porosity, sanding, application method, and product instructions. Always follow the label and test a small area.

FAQ

Common Deck Stain Calculator questions

Does this include railings?

No. Add railing, stairs, fascia, and underside area separately if you plan to coat them.

How many coats of deck stain do I need?

Follow the specific product label. Some coatings are one coat and some systems use multiple coats.
