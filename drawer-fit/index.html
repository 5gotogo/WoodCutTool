THE DIMENSION CHAIN

Opening. Box. Front.
Keep their references separate.

The mounting corridor

The runner lives between measured support planes. Front, middle and rear widths constrain the same constant box. Three acceptable total widths still need straight planes and verified individual side gaps.

The structural box

The outside width comes from the corridor and hardware range. This cut list uses full-length sides, front/back between them, and a bottom attached underneath. The total outside height includes that bottom.

The decorative front

The front group fills its own measured envelope. Edge gaps and intermediate reveals determine the finished faces. A neat elevation does not place the runners, locate fixings or prove handle and door clearance.

REPRODUCIBLE BENCH EXAMPLE

Three drawers.
One clear calculation trail.

A parallel 500 mm mounting corridor and illustrative 13 mm target give a 474 mm outside box. The 500 mm box length, 10 mm setback and 560 mm usable depth leave 50 mm behind it. Three equal bays in 600 mm provide 200 mm per bay before the entered box and clearance budgets.

With 12 mm walls, a 6 mm applied bottom and 150 mm total box height, wall parts are 144 mm high and the front/back are 450 mm long. A separate 536 × 636 mm envelope with 2 mm gaps yields 532 × 209.333 mm decorative fronts. All four physical checks start unchecked.

METHOD / VERSION 1

Know what the screen proves.

For measured mounting widths W, allowed per-side space [a, b] and a centered constant box B, every station requires W − 2b ≤ B ≤ W − 2a. The common interval is [max(W) − 2b, min(W) − 2a]. The chosen width is min(W) − 2 × target. The screen rejects an empty interval or a chosen width outside any entered station’s range.

Remaining rear space is usable depth − box-front setback − outside box length. Equal bay height is (clear stack height − (count − 1) × intermediate rail) ÷ count. Each box plus its entered above/below clearances must fit that bay. Equal front width is envelope width − left gap − right gap; height is (envelope height − top gap − bottom gap − (count − 1) × internal gap) ÷ count. Neither formula places the box relative to the front.

The screen does not infer hardware specifications, individual left/right alignment, drawer capacity, fixing strength, bottom strength, drilling positions or undermount preparation. Part sizes are finished dimensions without kerf, trim or edging deductions. A geometric pass requires physical verification of the actual assembly and hardware instructions.
