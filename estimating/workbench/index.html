How the number is calculated

Length × width × quantity gives net area. Add your allowance, divide by sheet area, then round up to whole sheets. Multiply by your sheet price and add hours × hourly cost.

A sheet count based on area is a minimum allowance, not a cut plan. It cannot prove that every part will pack.
