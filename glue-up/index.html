THE WORKSHOP METHOD / V1

Two clocks.
One first application.

Open used = spreading + mating. Total used = spreading + mating + pressure application. Both deadlines begin with the first adhesive in that batch. Margin = your limit − your reserve − the relevant duration.

Setup is completed before adhesive and shifts the schedule without consuming the glue budget. Independent batches run in series. A shared wet joint cannot restart its clock because it was moved to another row.

What do the source examples mean?

Open 4–6 min; total 10–15 min at 70°F / 50% RH. Open 8–10 min; total 20–25 min at 70°F / 50% RH. Loading a reference uses its lower endpoints. These are linked manufacturer reference conditions, not a prediction for your shop. Record the actual product, environment and process evidence before marking the conditions check.

When does a timing result ask for revision?

A negative margin means the entered sequence exceeds its limit after reserve. Exact zero is a planning boundary. Shorten and rehearse the sequence, or establish a supported alternative product limit. Positive timing margins leave the physical evidence checks separate; they do not prove a sound joint.

What happens after final pressure?

The calculated plan ends when final pressure is applied. Clamp holding, curing, handling and machining need their own product-specific instructions. Clamps remain occupied after a batch reaches its endpoint, so resource availability must be checked separately before starting the next assembly.

Which assemblies fit this model?

Use serial independent glue-ups with one first-adhesive reference and one final-pressure endpoint per row. The tool uses a conservative last-mating event for the whole batch. It does not solve parallel staffing, individual glue-line clocks, shared-clamp release, adhesive quantity, clamp force or structural capacity.

How do I preserve the draft?

Valid inputs save locally when browser storage is available. JSON preserves editable inputs; importing previews replacement before applying it. CSV records timing and preparation checks, including plans that need revision. If storage is unreadable, export its recovery file before explicitly replacing it.
