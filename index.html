BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

A few useful answers.

CutList support →
Does CutList offer a free trial?

Yes. CutList offers a 7-day free trial. After the trial, an active subscription is required to continue using the app. Current prices and trial terms are shown in the app before you subscribe.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. CutList processes your parts and layouts on your device. Your woodworking projects do not require an account or cloud upload.

Which devices can I use?

CutList is designed for iPhone and iPad. It requires iOS 17.6 or iPadOS 17.6 or later. Check the App Store for current device compatibility.

Can I try a calculator before downloading?

Yes. The free browser calculator can estimate sheet layouts, keep a draft in this browser, export CSV or JSON, and print a summary. Web projects do not automatically transfer to the app; re-create and verify your list in CutList.

Does a layout guarantee I can make every cut?

Always check your actual sheet sizes, saw kerf, grain, workholding and cutting sequence. Review the plan against your tools and material before cutting.
