How do I turn a real opening and cabinet design into parts that assemble and install correctly?

This hub is a navigation and decision surface. The pillar guide explains the full method, supporting guides isolate one input or workflow, Troubleshooting diagnoses failures, Checklists release irreversible work, Worksheets preserve project evidence, and calculators or apps apply the reviewed inputs.

1

Verify the opening

Measure finished references, limiting width and height, floor and wall variation, service zones, and neighboring work.

2

Choose construction and hardware

Resolve box style, backs, joinery, door condition, drawer slides, hinges, fillers, and finished-end requirements.

3

Calculate and reverse-check parts

Translate the approved envelope into parts, then rebuild the arithmetic to prove the assembly returns to the intended size.

4

Release one first article

Dry-fit or install one representative cabinet, drawer, or hardware set before multiplying repeated dimensions.

Evidence required

Make the result reproducible

Keep the measured opening, cabinet allocation, actual material thickness, construction section, hardware model, calculated parts, first-article result, and current drawing revision together.

Stop condition

Do not turn uncertainty into permission

Stop when the field reference is unfinished, hardware is unspecified, actual sheet thickness is unknown, or the part calculation cannot be reversed to the approved cabinet envelope.

Apply the method

Move from reading to a checked project action

Choose the surface matching the next decision. Calculators and apps test inputs, Worksheets retain project values, Checklists control release, Troubleshooting isolates disagreements, and source-linked examples or research make assumptions inspectable.

Questions before release

Cabinet Planning FAQ

Should cabinet parts be calculated from nominal plywood thickness?

No. Use the actual accepted material thickness whenever joinery, clear openings, drawer fit, or finished dimensions depend on it.

When can repeated cabinet dimensions be copied?

Only after the openings are verified as equal and one representative calculation or first article has passed.

Does a cabinet cut list replace field measurements?

No. It records the production result; the controlling finished opening and installation conditions still need direct verification.
