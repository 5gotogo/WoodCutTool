Make the result reproducible

Stop when a material group lacks a complete quantity basis, a supplier product is ambiguous, contingency has no stated purpose, or the received stock differs from the approved order.

Questions before release

Estimating and Purchasing FAQ

Why can the purchase quantity exceed the cut-list quantity?

Suppliers sell whole sheets, boards, packs, or minimum quantities, and the project may include documented contingency or stock replacement.

Is a waste percentage enough for ordering?

No. The order needs complete fit or another valid quantity basis plus real supplier units and usable stock assumptions.

When should received material create a new revision?

When product, size, thickness, condition, quantity, or approved substitution changes the production or layout basis.
