How do I keep shop output aligned with the real room, structure, services, and installation sequence?

This hub is a navigation and decision surface. The pillar guide explains the full method, supporting guides isolate one input or workflow, Troubleshooting diagnoses failures, Checklists release irreversible work, Worksheets preserve project evidence, and calculators or apps apply the reviewed inputs.

1

Measure finished conditions

Use shared datums and record width, height, depth, level, plumb, bow, obstructions, services, and pending finish work.

2

Allocate fit and adjustment

Define cabinet or assembly envelopes, fillers, scribes, clearances, leveling range, access, and approved field-adjustment limits.

3

Release the room package

Match physical items, labels, drawings, hardware, loose parts, installation references, and visible holds to one revision.

4

Close field changes

Capture the observed condition, affected parts, approved option, revised documents, and final installed evidence.

Evidence required

Make the result reproducible

The handoff should preserve measured conditions, marked datums, current drawings, room and part IDs, packing count, installation holds, approved changes, final observations, and the responsible acceptance record.

Stop condition

Do not turn uncertainty into permission

Stop when finished references are unavailable, structural or service conditions conflict with the plan, the current revision is unclear, or a field change has no approved owner and impact review.

Questions before release

Installation and Field Fit FAQ

Should unfinished site dimensions release final parts?

Only when the remaining finish buildup and responsibility are explicitly approved; otherwise keep the affected scope on hold.

What makes a field change controlled?

It identifies the observed fact, affected scope, approved option, owner, revised documents, and exact work being released.

Is delivery the same as installation handoff?

No. Delivery transfers custody; installation handoff also confirms revision, room allocation, references, holds, and usable package condition.
