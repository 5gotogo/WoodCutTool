How do I convert room geometry, tile size, pattern, cuts, and spare needs into a defensible order?

This hub is a navigation and decision surface. The pillar guide explains the full method, supporting guides isolate one input or workflow, Troubleshooting diagnoses failures, Checklists release irreversible work, Worksheets preserve project evidence, and calculators or apps apply the reviewed inputs.

1

Measure each surface

Record finished lengths, widths, heights, openings, niches, slopes, transitions, and areas that will not receive tile.

2

Define the layout

Choose tile orientation, pattern, grout joint, centerlines, starting points, edge-cut rules, and feature alignment.

3

Calculate purchasable quantity

Convert net coverage plus justified waste and spares into the manufacturer box coverage and whole-box order.

4

Verify before setting

Dry-lay or mark critical lines, confirm lot and condition, and resolve substrate, waterproofing, movement, and transition requirements.

Evidence required

Make the result reproducible

Keep the dimension sketch, surface breakdown, tile and lot identification, pattern, joint, calculated coverage, waste reason, box rounding, layout reference, attic stock, and approved installation specification.

Stop condition

Do not turn uncertainty into permission

Stop when substrate or waterproofing is unresolved, critical dimensions are missing, edge cuts are unacceptable, box coverage is unclear, or ordered lots cannot be kept consistent.

Apply the method

Move from reading to a checked project action

Choose the surface matching the next decision. Calculators and apps test inputs, Worksheets retain project values, Checklists control release, Troubleshooting isolates disagreements, and source-linked examples or research make assumptions inspectable.

Questions before release

Tile Planning FAQ

Is a fixed tile waste percentage always enough?

No. Pattern, room shape, tile size, breakage, edge cuts, lot availability, and attic-stock needs can change the allowance.

Should openings always be subtracted from wall tile?

Only when the resulting offcuts and layout genuinely reduce purchasable coverage; small openings may not reduce whole-box demand.

Does the calculator verify the substrate?

No. Surface preparation, waterproofing, movement joints, setting materials, and code requirements need separate current guidance.
