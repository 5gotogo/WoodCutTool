Casework Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Casework handles cabinet projects, client and shop details, measurements, material and hardware data, calculations, and PDF or CSV exports on iPhone and iPad.

Effective Date: July 27, 2026 | Last Updated: July 27, 2026

Casework is designed as a local-first cabinet planning tool. Its core project, calculation, nesting, cutting-guide, and document-generation workflows run on your device. Casework does not require you to create a developer account or upload projects to a Casework cloud service.

Information You Enter or Create

You may enter or create project names, project mode, client names, job-site details, notes, cabinet types, quantities, dimensions, units, construction and joinery settings, material names, sheet sizes, thicknesses, grain settings, prices, hardware names, quantities, prices, shop name, phone number, email address, shop address, and display preferences. The app uses this information to save your projects, calculate cabinet parts, estimate material and hardware needs, prepare sheet layouts and cutting steps, and create the exports you request.

You decide what to enter. Client, job-site, shop, and contact fields are optional; avoid entering information that is not needed for your planning or exported document.

On-Device Processing and Local Storage

Casework stores saved project records in its private application-support area on your device. App preferences and the optional shop profile used for PDF letterhead are also stored locally. Part decomposition, material matching, nesting, cost calculations, cutting-sequence generation, and PDF or CSV creation are performed on the device.

The current Casework release does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not operate a developer-hosted service that receives your projects, measurements, client details, shop profile, calculation results, or export files.

Accounts, Advertising, and Tracking

Casework does not require an account, email sign-in, password, or team workspace for its core features. The app is not built around advertising profiles, third-party data brokerage, public project sharing, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your personal information.

PDF and CSV Exports

When you choose to export, Casework creates a temporary PDF or CSV file on the device. The PDF may contain the project name, client details, job-site details, notes, shop letterhead information, calculated parts, material and hardware estimates, sheet diagrams, and review notes. The CSV contains cut-list information. Review the file before sharing, especially if you entered client or business information.

The file is passed to the iOS share sheet only after you choose an export action. You then decide whether to save it to Files, print it, send it, or provide it to another app or service. Apple, the selected destination, storage provider, printer, and recipient may process or retain the file under their own terms and privacy policies. Copies outside Casework are not controlled or automatically deleted by Casework.

Permissions and Device Access

  • Local app storage: used to save projects, cabinet settings, material and hardware libraries, shop profile information, and app preferences.
  • File sharing, printing, or destination access: used through iOS only when you choose where to send or save an exported PDF or CSV.
  • Camera, photo library, microphone, contacts, and location: the current Casework release does not request these permissions for its cabinet-planning workflow.
  • Apple-managed backups: depending on your device and Apple account settings, locally stored app data may be included in an encrypted device or iCloud backup managed by Apple.

External Links and Services

Casework includes a link to Apple's standard End User License Agreement. Opening an external link leaves the app and is handled by Apple, the browser, or the destination service according to its own privacy practices. Casework does not receive the browsing information those external services may collect.

Data Retention and Deletion

Project records remain in Casework's local storage until you delete them or remove the app. You can delete individual projects from the project picker and clear optional shop-profile fields in Settings. Removing Casework from the device deletes its local app container, subject to any Apple-managed backups you have enabled.

Exported or shared files must be deleted separately from Files, email, messages, cloud storage, another app, a printer queue, a recipient's device, or any other destination where you placed them. To manage or delete an Apple backup, use your Apple account and device backup settings.

Data Security

Keeping project data in the app's local container reduces routine transmission, but no device or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device with a passcode, keep iOS updated, limit access to unlocked devices, and review sensitive client or job-site details before exporting or sharing. You are responsible for securing copies you send to other destinations.

Children's Privacy

Casework is a woodworking and cabinet-planning utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a Casework account or developer-hosted project service because those services are not provided.
