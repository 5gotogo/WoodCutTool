JobPhotoLog Support

Support for JobPhotoLog, an iPhone app for keeping project photos, before-and-after records, client signatures, and job-site follow-up items together.

App Overview

JobPhotoLog helps contractors, tradespeople, property managers, and project teams document work in progress. Create a project, capture or import photos, group them into a clear record, compare before and after conditions, collect a signature when appropriate, and share a report when you choose.

CategoryProductivity / Utilities
PlatformiOS (iPhone)
Version1.0.0
PrivacyProject records kept on device

Main Features

  • Create and organize projects by client, location, status, or job name.
  • Capture photos with the camera or import existing images for a project record.
  • Track photo count, elapsed job time, and project progress from one project screen.
  • Build before-and-after comparisons to show changes clearly.
  • Record job-site follow-up or corrective-action items in a project checklist.
  • Collect an optional client or site signature.
  • Prepare a photo-based report and use the iOS share sheet when you choose to send, print, or export it.

Core User Flow

Step 1Create a project and add its identifying details.
Step 2Choose the project, then capture or import photos as work progresses.
Step 3Add comparisons, follow-up items, or a signature, then review and share a report when ready.

Camera, Photo Library, and Signature Help

JobPhotoLog asks for camera access only when you choose to take a photo. It may ask for photo-library access only when you choose to import or save images. Signature input is used to capture the mark you create in the app for the selected project; it does not verify a person's identity or authority.

Reports and Sharing

Reports are built from the project content you select. When you choose a system share, print, or export action, iOS sends the selected report or images to the destination you choose. Check the recipient, destination app, printer, and attachment contents before sharing, because those services handle information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Privacy and Data Handling

JobPhotoLog is designed to keep project records on your device for its core workflow and does not require an account. See the JobPhotoLog Privacy Policy for details about photos, project details, permissions, data deletion, and the app disclaimer.

Support Contact

For help, bug reports, or App Review clarification, contact the developer with the app version and a short description of the issue. Do not send confidential project photos or customer information unless it is necessary to explain the issue.
