ThumbType Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ThumbType handles typing practice text, test results, daily challenge progress, streaks, and app preferences on iPhone.

Effective Date: July 4, 2026 | Last Updated: July 4, 2026

ThumbType is an iOS app for phone typing practice, typing speed tests, accuracy feedback, spelling exercises, daily challenges, and progress tracking. The core app experience is designed to run on your device.

Information You Enter or Create

You may type practice text inside exercises and create practice results such as WPM, accuracy, session counts, best scores, streaks, challenge completion status, language preferences, and general app settings. This information is used to provide typing practice, progress tracking, and app customization features.

Local Storage

Your practice history, progress statistics, streaks, challenge status, selected language, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, locally stored app data may be removed unless it is included in Apple-managed device backups.

Typed Practice Content

ThumbType is for typing text shown inside the app's practice and test screens. The app is not designed to collect passwords, private messages, payment details, or other sensitive text. Please do not type sensitive personal information into practice fields.

Network and Accounts

The core typing practice workflow does not require a ThumbType account or a remote profile service. We do not operate a public profile system, social feed, leaderboard, or cloud typing-history storage service for ThumbType.

Keyboard and Device Features

ThumbType uses the iOS keyboard or text input interface when you type during an exercise. Practice results may be affected by iOS keyboard settings such as autocorrect, predictive text, language keyboard, and accessibility settings. ThumbType does not need access to your messages, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera for the core workflow described in this policy.

Exporting and Sharing

If the app offers an export, screenshot, share, backup, or system share-sheet action and you choose to use it, the selected content is passed to the iOS destination that you explicitly select. That destination may process the information according to its own privacy policy.

What We Do Not Sell

We do not sell your personal information. ThumbType is not designed around advertising profiles, third-party data brokerage, social feeds, or public user-generated content.

Permissions

  • Text input: used so you can complete typing practice, speed tests, spelling exercises, and daily challenges.
  • On-device storage: used to save practice results, progress, streaks, language preferences, and app settings.
  • Notifications: used only if the app offers practice reminders and you choose to enable them through iOS.
  • Apple-managed backups: controlled through your Apple account and device settings.

Disclaimer

ThumbType is a practice and measurement tool for personal use. WPM, accuracy, spelling feedback, streaks, and progress summaries are estimates and may not reflect standardized testing conditions, professional keyboarding assessment, school requirements, job requirements, or accessibility needs. We do not guarantee any specific improvement in typing speed, accuracy, spelling, productivity, academic performance, or work performance. You are responsible for how you use the app and for confirming whether its results are suitable for your own purpose.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can adjust or delete practice results, progress records, and app settings from within the app where controls are available. You may also remove the app from your device to delete locally stored data, subject to any Apple-managed backups you have enabled.

Children's Privacy

ThumbType is intended for general iPhone users and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can help review the concern.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or when privacy, platform, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised last updated date.
