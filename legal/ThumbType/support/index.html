ThumbType Support

Support page for ThumbType, an iPhone app for phone typing practice, speed tests, accuracy tracking, spelling exercises, and daily challenges.

App Overview

ThumbType helps iPhone users build faster and more accurate phone typing habits through short practice sessions, a 60-second typing test, daily mixed exercises, and progress statistics. It is designed for self-guided practice on a phone screen.

CategoryEducation / Utilities
PlatformiOS (iPhone)
Version1.0.0
PrivacyOn-device practice and progress data

Main Features

  • Continue short typing practice sessions focused on speed and accuracy.
  • Take a 60-second typing test to estimate words per minute and typing accuracy.
  • Complete a daily challenge with a fresh mixed exercise.
  • Track sessions today, best WPM, accuracy, streaks, and progress trends.
  • Practice spelling and phone keyboard familiarity with guided text prompts.
  • Change app language from the in-app language selector when supported.

Core User Flow

Step 1Open ThumbType and choose Continue Practice, 60-Second Typing Test, or Daily Challenge.
Step 2Type the displayed prompt as accurately as possible using the iPhone keyboard.
Step 3Review WPM, accuracy, session count, streak, and progress statistics after practice.

Practice Results

WPM, accuracy, streaks, and progress values are intended for personal practice feedback. Results may vary depending on keyboard settings, autocorrect, device size, prompt difficulty, language, interruptions, and typing conditions.

Privacy and Data Handling

ThumbType is designed around local practice data and on-device app settings. The core app workflow does not require an account. See the ThumbType Privacy Policy.
