How the estimate works

Use the number as a planning starting point.

Side panels use full height and depth. The top, bottom, and shelves fit between sides; the back is sized from the overall cabinet dimensions. The sheet estimate uses total panel area plus waste.

FAQ

Common Cabinet Cut List Calculator questions

Can I use this for face-frame cabinets?

Use it as a rough starting point only. Face frames, overlays, reveals, and rail/stile dimensions need their own design rules.

Does it optimize plywood sheets?

It estimates panel area. Open the Plywood Cut Calculator or CutList after you confirm the finished part dimensions.
