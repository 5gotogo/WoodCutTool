How the estimate works

Use the number as a planning starting point.

The calculator subtracts the square post volume from a cylindrical hole volume, then converts the result into cubic feet, cubic yards, and a conservative bag estimate.

Measure first

FAQ

Common Post Hole Concrete Calculator questions

How many concrete bags fit in a post hole?

It depends on the net hole volume after the post displaces material. The calculator shows an estimate for the bag weight you choose.

Should I add waste to concrete?

Yes. A small allowance helps account for irregular holes, spillage, and mixing loss.
